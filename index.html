Making language models smaller without losing the languages that need them most.

About

MS in Computer Science at BYU, graduating December 2026. I work in the machine translation lab on model compression: pruning, quantization, distillation, and what each one costs a language that had little training data to begin with.

Now: why does 4-bit quantization hurt low-resource languages more than English? I am testing whether those languages are under-trained or whether the damage sits in specific parts of the network, with RTN, GPTQ, AWQ and SmoothQuant implemented from the papers.

  • Pruned and quantized Aya Expanse 8B to 3.3x smaller at 1.8x vLLM throughput, within 0.8% COMET. Three models on Hugging Face.
  • Speech-to-speech translation for Efik, Igbo, Swahili and Xhosa, with a speech-quality metric distilled for languages BLASER did not cover.
  • Before the MS: three years of production engineering, most recently the AWS platform for a live AI dubbing service.

I build with coding agents daily, under tests and review, and I only ship what I can explain.
